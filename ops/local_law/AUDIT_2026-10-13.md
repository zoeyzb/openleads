# Mac law lead pipeline — evidence audit (2026-10-13)

## Operational goal and count discipline

Primary calling-qualified contact = genuine US private law firm, independently supported **firm-wide** 2–10 attorneys, no owned website after trustworthy checks, and attributable published US business phone. Email is a **bonus for calling** and is a separate strict-email qualification subset. Source listings, phone-only Maps hints, and jurisdiction-only attorney counts are **research**, not call-ready.

## Current software and remaining blocker

Local working-tree changes (not yet synced to remote at document creation because Mac connector dropped) include:
1. A review state for jurisdiction-only headcounts, and stronger non-law employer screening.
2. Firm+geography website query rather than exact unformatted phone.
3. A real CLI site-provider preflight that does **not** spend candidates while known-owned-site positive control fails.
4. Defensive final CSV count/export requiring independently evidenced size, phone, completed site checks, and recent proof rather than trusting a status label.
5. Source-query controller classifies zero-yield subprocess exits as failed acquisition evidence rather than success.
6. Read-only-research archive indexing and stable deduplication.

The main remaining blocks are: a **productive and authorized firm-wide 2–10 source**; a **usable website-check source that finds its own known-positive controls** and safely distinguishes owned sites from third-party listings; and a downstream Sheet writer authorized to write the phone-qualified output. Merely activating more forks, increasing concurrency, or searching more cities cannot repair those missing proofs.

## Minimal architecture

`source-specific adapters -> evidence normalization/deduplication in SQLite -> firmwide size and real-practice verification -> positive owned-site exclusion -> controlled independent absence search -> calling-qualified export -> Sheets`.

Keep a separate `pending/review/rejected` research queue; don't turn raw records into eligible leads. Operationally, one Python application, one local SQLite source of truth (legacy archives stay cold), launchd scheduler, and the already-connected Sheets integration are sufficient. Crawl4AI/Scrapling/Maps code should be optional adapters for a demonstrably productive source, **not extra always-on services**.
