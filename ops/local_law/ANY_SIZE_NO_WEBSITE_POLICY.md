# SUPERSEDED — strict law lead eligibility

Current project requirement: an eligible lead must be a real private law firm with **firmwide 2–10 attorneys**, **no owned website after independent verification**, and a **source-verified usable email**. A published business phone is retained for identity and outreach but does not replace the email requirement.

State/jurisdiction attorney counts, missing Maps website fields, phone-only records, and operator website reviews are research evidence only. They cannot enter the eligible count until all three hard gates above pass.
