# OpenLeads deep verification repair — 2026-10-19

## Contract

Strictly qualified: real active U.S. private law practice; identity and source-published business phone matched; two independent healthy website-search controls and no attributable owned website after detailed screening; source-published email and verified MX; source evidence retained; no duplicate phone/email. Attorney count is **never** a filter (including solos/unknown size). Phone-only prospects stay in a separate export. A completed negative web search is a bounded assessment, not proof of nonexistence; MX indicates domain routing, not recipient deliverability.

## Measured root causes

- At the start of the deep repair the canonical queue had **four** strict email leads, **ten** separate phone-only leads, and **1,086** website review holds. A large review count does not mean 1,086 correctable false positives. Many hits are real attributable domains or unresolved website identity.
- The research catalog contains **9,037** historical source-research rows but only **one** source-bonus-email row; those legacy records are not 9,037 publicly email-verified leads. The PLEA roster has **334** email-published firm groups, most with custom-domain addresses and substantial owned-site signals. Mere raw discovery cannot fill strict exports.
- Before repair, the canonical queue permanently retained earlier website holds even where *every retained search hit was an independently identified third-party directory*. Adding a directory to the classification list could not clear historical holds. Corrected with tightly scoped, independently rerun reviews, a seven-day cooldown, audit history, and no automatic clearance of uncertain/direct owned-site URLs.
- PLEA's bounded site-audit queue prioritized multi-member firms ahead of published personal mailboxes. This contradicted the removal of attorney-size gating. Site-audit ordering is now source-mailbox first irrespective of attorney count; the hourly bounded allowance increased to 12. No PLEA website research status alone creates a qualified lead.
- The new Wisconsin local-bar official-public-contacts source (robots permitted) publishes current named officers, firm affiliation, phone, mailbox, and current term within a single card. Added per-card exact identity/phone/email validation, active-term and private-practice checks, official-source digest, daily crawl cooldown, idempotent SQLite import and the existing canonical two-provider website verification. Live initial pilot yielded **62** scoped public contact cards and **55** *new unverified* queue candidates, **zero** direct qualifications. First 32 canonical WI-source verification attempts produced **30 website holds** and **two inconclusive**, no strict lead. Two accidentally admitted government district attorney employer rows were rejected with evidence-preserving holds; regression tests prevent recurrence.
- User-data database backups and append-only observation history were preserved. Local Mac SQLite, hourly launchd, local SearXNG engines, Maps research and strict CRM integration remain unchanged in hosting and billing. No Railway/Render, simulated emails or test contacts were exported.

## Site-evidence review safety

An archived `review_website` row enters automatic review *only* if **every** retained candidate URL is a recognized third-party listing/social/editorial domain, there is no direct source-owned-site hint, no adverse hold, and historic checked providers have not failed. The original result never independently certifies no website. A **new** canonical source fetch and healthy two-provider site search must pass all strict export gates; otherwise the historical hold remains. An unknown/owned domain or previously confirmed owned site cannot be auto-cleared. Government/non-law or inactive evidence can reject previously held records. Recheck attempts have a dedicated SQLite cooldown table.

## Verification and limitations

Unit tests cover old site-hold retry safety (including legacy observations), unknown/owned-site exclusion, government and generic-employer filters, Wisconsin contact isolation and term dates, robots opt-out, idempotence, source-first email prioritization and retained count-independent qualification. Repo CI and local full suites are required before publication.

The number **10,000** is an acquisition target, not a truthful current total. The source-published email + no-owned-site intersection is rare in the measured pools. Next efforts must increase **different** publicly attributable, active private-practice contacts with usable published emails and no-site screening, rather than renaming phone-only/raw records as eligible. Search engines, official rosters, upstream access, and user Mac availability impose limits.
